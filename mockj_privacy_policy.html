MockJ Privacy Policy 🛡️

Effective Date: May 20, 2024

Last Updated: February 2025

At MockJ (a project by MoreiraJ and MLTX Studio), we prioritize your privacy as much as your success. This policy breaks down how we handle your data across our AI and asset platforms at mockj.online.

1. DATA WE COLLECT 🔍

  • Account Info: Your name, email, and social login data (via Privy/Google/Discord) used to manage your MockJ subscription.
  • Blockchain Data: Your public wallet address.
    ✨ Note: We never ask for, store, or have access to your private keys or seed phrases.
  • Identity Data (KYC): For high-stakes transactions (Real Estate/RWA), identity verification is handled by our partner Sumsub. MockJ does not store your raw ID documents; we only receive a "Green" or "Red" status signal.
  • Usage Data: Prompts, AI interactions, and image/video generation history (unless deleted by the user).

2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA 🧠

  • Service Delivery: To power your AI chats, deep reasoning, and asset closings.
  • Compliance: To ensure we aren't facilitating transactions for sanctioned individuals (AML/OFAC compliance).
  • Optimization: To improve the MockJ engine and personality presets.

3. DATA SHARING & THIRD PARTIES 🤝

We don't sell your data to advertisers. Period. We only share data with essential infrastructure partners:

  • OnSpace/Google: For powering AI reasoning and generation.
  • Stripe: For secure subscription billing (MockJ Pro/Intro).
  • Blockchain Networks: Transaction data is permanently recorded on public ledgers (Polygon/Ethereum) by nature. This cannot be deleted.

4. YOUR RIGHTS 🔓

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR/CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Request Deletion: You can wipe your chat history and account data from our servers.
  • Export Data: You can export your chat and transaction history.
  • Opt-Out: You can choose not to engage in KYC-required features, though this will limit your ability to purchase real-world assets.

Important Notice on Permanent Ledger Records (Real Estate / RWA) 🏛️

Please be aware that while we can delete your account information, email, and chat history from our centralized databases upon request, any transaction data recorded on a blockchain (such as property tokenization, asset transfers, or smart contract interactions) is permanently and immutably stored on a public ledger. A blockchain transaction is forever and cannot be altered or deleted by MockJ or any other party.

5. SECURITY 🛡️

MockJ uses industry-standard encryption (AES-256) and secure API tunnels. However, the security of your crypto assets ultimately depends on your own wallet hygiene. Always use a hardware wallet for high-value assets.

For privacy-related inquiries or to exercise your data rights, contact the crew at MLTX Studio via the help portal at mockj.online or email us directly.
